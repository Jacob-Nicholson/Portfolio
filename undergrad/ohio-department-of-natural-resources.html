ODNR Internship

Work from my 2025 summer internship as a graphic designer for the Ohio Department of Natural Resources. During my time in the office of Creative Services, I designed permanent signage, murals, flyers, stickers, and wayfindning for Ohio State Parks and the Ohio State Fair.

I had the chance to design this seven foot mural made from thousands of LEGOS to celebrate Ohio’s nature, and I volunteered to help visitors at the fair put it together. I believed the mural needed to be busy and full of color, as every inch of it needed to be interesting to put together. This work later went on to become part of the Imagination Station’s Traveling Bricks exhibition.
