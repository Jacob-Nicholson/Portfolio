VCD BFA SHOWCASE Website

I designed and developed this showcase website to go alongside our in-person gallery and collect KSU’s Senior design talent in one place. Working from branding and photography created by my classmates, I built a fully responsive site using HTML, CSS, P5.JS, and JavaScript. This custom solution provided a faster, more cost-effective alternative to modern web builders, helping our program stand out from competitors while giving students better control over their portfolio presentation.
