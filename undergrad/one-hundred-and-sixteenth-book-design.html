One Hundred and Sixteenth: Book Design

One hundred and Sixteenth is a self-bound designed book for Kantanji Brown Jackson, the first black woman to be elected into the supreme court. The book features an info-graphic cover made from acrylic which is filled with colored beads representing Supreme Court Justice’s race and gender throughout history. The cover allows users to interact with the book by rolling the beads inside around to reveal the information the page behind.
