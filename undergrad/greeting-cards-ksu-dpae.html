Greeting Cards - KSU DPAE

The Division of Philanthropy and Alumni Engagement at Kent State University regularly sends greeting cards from the President to the school’s most generous donors. I had the chance to collaborate with their marketing team to create several refined and printed cards throughout one semester. Due to the context of who was sending and receiving these cards, it was important they had a "presidential" atmosphere to them.

This card above was formatted with the glitter on a separate layer so it could be printed in gold foil.
