Brand Identity- Dance Festival

A brand identity for an imagined dance festival in Charlotte, North Carolina. I tried to create a set of motifs that would visualize the movement and energy of dance, and that could be repeated to fit the different needs of the brand. The resulting product is this series of posters, website, logos, lanyards and badges.
